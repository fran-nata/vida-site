We're often asked why we don't work with this or that well-known brand — the answer mostly comes down to what we install and warranty every day. Several architectural window film brands exist on the market, including 3M and Tekton Performance Films. Here's what sets these premium products apart.

## Thermal performance

Both brands offer lineups with excellent solar heat rejection. Tekton stands out with newer technologies optimized for the North American climate, paired with very fine optical clarity.

## Why Vida Vitres works with Tekton

We're an official Tekton Performance Films partner for their value for money, manufacturer warranty, and full product range — from Clarion 70 to Nocturna, including silver and bronze reflective finishes.

## What actually matters

Beyond the brand name, installation quality remains the biggest factor in how long a film lasts. A poorly executed install can shorten the lifespan of any product, no matter the brand.
