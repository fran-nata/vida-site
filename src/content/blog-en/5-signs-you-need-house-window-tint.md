Most people who call us don't say "I need house window tint" — they say "something's not right about this room." Not sure if window tint is right for your home? Here are five signs the time has come.

- **Some rooms become uncomfortable in the afternoon** — especially if they face south or west.
- **You keep closing the curtains** to avoid glare on your screens.
- **Your furniture or floors show signs of fading** near the windows.
- **Your air conditioning runs more than it used to** just to keep things comfortable.
- **You have a sunroom or solarium** that's hard to use in summer.

Unlike replacing your windows, house window tint installs in a single day, with no mess and no major disruption to your routine.
