It's one of the questions we hear most, almost always asked with a hint of embarrassment: "can people actually see in at night?" The bathroom is one of the rooms where privacy matters most — and where curtains aren't always practical because of the humidity. Frosted privacy window film is an elegant, durable alternative.

## How it works

The film diffuses light rather than blocking it: you see a blurred silhouette through the glass, never any detail, day or night with the lights on.

## Moisture resistance

Unlike some fabric curtains or blinds, our films are built to withstand a bathroom's constant humidity without peeling or growing mold.

## Finish options

- Uniform frosted — discreet and timeless
- Decorative patterns or bands
- Targeted zones (bottom half of the window only, for example)
