A building manager once told us he just wanted it to "look less 1998" — with no renovation budget. That's exactly the kind of problem frosted film solves. Renovating office partitions is expensive and slow. Frosted decorative film delivers a professional result at a fraction of the time and cost.

## An instant modern look

Applied to existing glass partitions, frosted film immediately gives a workspace a more upscale, current look, with no construction involved.

## Defining space without closing it off

Ideal for conference rooms and open-plan offices, it creates a visual separation while keeping the natural light flowing between spaces.

## Customization options

- Horizontal bands or geometric patterns
- Custom-cut company logo
- Targeted frosted zones for privacy (meeting rooms)
