"How much for the whole house?" — that's almost always the first question, and the honest answer is: it depends, and here's why. The price of house window tint installation varies a lot from one project to the next. Here are the factors that shape the final cost, and why a personalized estimate is still the best way to know your price.

## Factors that shape the price

- Total glass area to cover
- Type of film chosen (heat control, security, dual-purpose)
- Window accessibility (height, obstacles)
- Residential or commercial
