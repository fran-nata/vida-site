After a break-in nearby, many homeowners' instinct is to want to replace everything with tempered glass — until they see the bill. Replacing every window with tempered glass is expensive. Security window film offers an effective alternative: it reinforces your existing glass without major work.

## How it protects

The film is a tough polymer layer applied to the inside face of the glass. On impact, it holds glass fragments together instead of letting them fly apart — which significantly slows down a forced-entry attempt.

## Common applications

- Patio doors and glass front entry doors
- Ground-floor and basement windows
- Storefronts and commercial doors

## Security without sacrificing looks

Once installed, security window film is nearly invisible on clear glass — your windows keep their usual appearance.
