We've seen it at plenty of clients' homes: a hardwood floor that's perfectly even, except for one paler strip exactly where a rug sat for ten years. The sun coming through your windows doesn't just heat the room — it slowly fades your floors, furniture, and fabrics. UV window film tackles that cause directly.

## Why UV rays fade your interiors

UV rays break down pigments and fibers over time. A hardwood floor exposed for several hours a day can visibly change shade within a few years.

## A nearly invisible barrier

Our films block up to 99% of UV rays while staying transparent. You keep the view and the brightness, without the premature wear on your interiors.

## Where to install it first

- Living and dining rooms facing south or west
- Offices with wood or leather furniture
- Sunrooms and solariums with heavy exposure
